What to pick

Rating is the save

Up and love already file a song in Liked songs. Say so with the toast instead of adding a heart, which would be a fourth love.

Add to playlist: in the menu, and worded where there's room

In ⋯ everywhere, in the toast, and as a quiet seal in Now Playing's header. Never a bare + and never beside the marks. A long press on the tray can be a shortcut, never the only way.

Every view and toggle is worded or stamped

Lyrics and Queue keep their words at every width above a phone. Shuffle and repeat show "on" as a pressed stamp. No round hovers anywhere.
